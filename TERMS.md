# Last Title Standing Terms of Service

**Effective date:** 16 August 2026
**Publication status:** Review draft for current source version `1.5.1`; public
publication and installed-version status remain separate release gates

These terms govern use of Last Title Standing, a Reddit-native movie and TV
debate game. By using the app, you agree to use it lawfully, follow Reddit's
rules and the rules of the installing subreddit, and respect the moderators
who operate each community.

## The service

Last Title Standing lets a creator publish a Reddit game post with two opening
TMDB titles. Redditors vote between the current champion and challenger. After
each round, the community can nominate and select the next challenger. The app
stores the game state in Devvit-hosted services and uses scheduled jobs to close
rounds.

The app does not guarantee that every game will be available at every moment.
TMDB, Reddit, Reddit-hosted media, scheduled jobs, and Devvit infrastructure
can be unavailable or can change. A moderator may pause, end, remove, or
retire a game when necessary.

## Accounts and user actions

You must use a Reddit account to vote, nominate a title, create a game, or
manage a game. You are responsible for actions made through your account.

Votes and challenger selections are intentionally locked per Reddit account
and matchup. Do not evade those limits, manipulate the queue, submit abusive
titles, or use automation to interfere with a game. A game creator may manage
their own game; subreddit moderators may manage every game in their subreddit.

Creating a game is a separate, explicit action. The app shows the resolved
opening titles and creator attribution before confirmation. When you confirm,
the app may submit the new Reddit post on your behalf using the Reddit account
that confirmed the action. Installation or opening the app on a post alone
never creates or publishes a game.

## Community moderation

The installing subreddit controls its creation policy and community rules.
Moderators can choose moderators-only, approved-creator, or everyone creation;
pause or end games; and remove nominations with a reason. Approving or
revoking creators and reviewing the moderation audit log are planned for the
next phase. Backend support exists, but Creator Studio currently provides game
creation only and does not yet expose those moderator controls. Reddit's
normal report tools and the subreddit's Modmail are the current report and
support routes while the in-app report control is being completed.

Do not use the app to create or amplify harmful, illegal, sexually explicit,
harassing, hateful, deceptive, or otherwise prohibited content. A moderator or
Reddit may remove content or restrict access to the app.
